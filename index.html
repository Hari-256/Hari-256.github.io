01 · About me

Engineering the foundation other teams build on

I'm a Software Engineer with 5 years of experience in Java-based frameworks, microservices, cloud-native applications and Identity & Access Management (IAM/PAM) architecture. At Nokia, I design, optimize and maintain core internal software infrastructure and the secure access-control components that protect it.

My day-to-day includes critical business logic written with advanced Java features, secure internal REST APIs, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), and tracking tricky authentication and redirection issues down to their root cause. I work hands-on with Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, automation scripting, and Windows, Linux and RHEL servers.

I speak English (full professional), Telugu (native) and Hindi (professional working proficiency).
